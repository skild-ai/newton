# Wrecking crane

The procedural crane is scaled by two for the tabletop demonstration. The
robot pinches the original rear counterweight with stock fingers; there is no
added grasp handle or jaw extension. The slew joint's dry brake resists the
ball back-driving the cab after release.

The visual cab glazing, grille, track rollers, tread details, boom hoses,
beacon, and fasteners come from the detailed crane we built for the Steel
launch video scene. These 138 additional mesh shapes have no collision API and
do not contribute mass or inertia. The original 44 collision shapes and
authored inertial properties are preserved.

The crane and its visual details were procedurally generated by the Skild AI
team with in-house code. The creator confirmed in skild-ai/newton#27 that the
asset is Skild AI's and that no third-party models, textures, fonts or
reference geometry were used.
