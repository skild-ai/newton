# Rack plate

Dinner plate placed in the rack by `steel_plate_rack`. Procedurally generated
by the Skild AI team with an in-house generator. The USD holds the visual
surface, convex collision pieces, and the authored mass properties.

The creator confirmed in skild-ai/newton#27 that the asset is Skild AI's and
that no third-party models, textures, fonts or reference geometry were used.
