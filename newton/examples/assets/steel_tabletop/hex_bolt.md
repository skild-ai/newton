# Hex bolt

M8-style hex bolt used by `steel_bolt_sweep`. Procedurally generated by the
Skild AI team with an in-house generator. The USD holds the visual thread
surface, convex collision pieces for the shank and head, and the authored mass
properties. Thread-scale collision is omitted.
