# Unpacking mug

Procedurally generated mug from the `drinkware_studio` family of the Skild AI
team's in-house generator, as used in the Steel launch video scene: seed 4,
straight profile, loop handle, flat foot, blue glaze. The USD preserves the
authored visual surface and all convex collision pieces. Dimensions are scaled
by 0.85, mass by 0.85 cubed, and inertia by 0.85 to the fifth power. The
resulting mass is 0.138959 kg. The open cup and handle retain their cavities;
they are not replaced by a single convex hull.

The creator confirmed in skild-ai/newton#27 that the asset is Skild AI's and
that no third-party models, textures, fonts or reference geometry were used.
