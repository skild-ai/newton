# Unpacking mug

Procedurally generated mug from the Skild AI team's in-house generator, as used
in the Steel launch video scene: seed 4, straight profile, loop handle, flat
foot, blue glaze. The USD preserves the authored visual surface and all convex
collision pieces. Dimensions are scaled by 0.85, mass by 0.85 cubed, and
inertia by 0.85 to the fifth power. The resulting mass is 0.138959 kg. The open
cup and handle retain their cavities; they are not replaced by a single convex
hull.
