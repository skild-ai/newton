The native Steel `franka_cable_ik_pick_place` and `franka_bimanual_cable` examples use the propagation contact response from 256 worlds (8 substeps of 16 iterations for one arm, 24 iterations for two) and the immediate response below; `--contact-response` still chooses either. The bimanual knot keeps the immediate response. The native `mujoco_franka_vbd_cable_admm_solver` example switches to the propagation response from 1,024 worlds.
