Add `SolverSteel(velocity_terms_theta=...)`, the weight of the end-of-step velocity in the Coriolis, centrifugal and gyroscopic forces of articulations, in first-order form: each step's `-theta * dt * H^-1 (dc/dqd) (qd - qd_start)` enters the next step's prediction, so the constraint solve keeps loop closures and contacts on their velocity targets. The default `0.0` keeps the explicit bias; `1.0` matches MuJoCo's `implicit` integrator. Both leave a fast closed loop with a frequency error first order in the step, of equal and opposite sign; `0.5` cancels it. On a four-bar swinging for 10 s at a 1.04 ms step the crank angle error falls from 17 deg to 0.6 deg.
