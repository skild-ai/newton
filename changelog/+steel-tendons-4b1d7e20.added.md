Add experimental MuJoCo-style tendons to `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`: fixed and spatial tendons from the `mujoco:tendon_*` attributes, with range limits and friction loss as constraint rows, and armature, stiffness/damping springs and tendon actuators folded into the mass matrix (`tendon_passive_mode`). `SolverSteel.tendon_lengths()`, `tendon_forces()` and `tendon_actuator_forces()` report lengths and forces. Contacts respond through the tendon mass terms under both `articulated_contact_response` values. See the tendon section of the MuJoCo solver guide for the supported features and limits.
