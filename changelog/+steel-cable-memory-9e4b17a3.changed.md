`SolverSteel` allocates per-body, per-row and per-contact buffers only where a kernel of the configuration reads them: rod trees skip the dense mass-matrix, factor and row blocks no dense row reaches, the branch accumulators of chain-only trees, world inertias without a dense mass matrix or free bodies, the per-rod material blocks, the friction-patch history under point friction and the restitution and momentum scratch the default path does not read. The rod factor prelude forms its inertias from the model's masses. Results are bitwise unchanged.
