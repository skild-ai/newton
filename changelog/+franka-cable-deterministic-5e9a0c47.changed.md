The native Steel `franka_cable_ik_pick_place` and `mujoco_franka_vbd_cable_admm_solver` examples run Steel's `RUN_TO_RUN` deterministic mode over a deterministic collision pipeline, so every replicated world grasps and places the cable alike (bitwise) at no measurable cost; before, rounding differences between worlds let a few of 256 or 2,048 worlds drop the cable in the marginal grasp. `cable_solver.make_solver` takes `deterministic`.
