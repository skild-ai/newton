Document that ``SolverSteel(row_schedule="contact_then_internal")`` does not feed internal-row corrections back into another contact sweep, so a contact load carried by a joint limit or another internal row can be left with substantial penetration, and a heavy enough body falls through; more iterations within each phase do not restore the interleaving. Use the default ``"interleaved"`` or ``"phased"`` schedule when contacts load internal rows.
