Document that ``SolverSteel(row_schedule="contact_then_internal")`` cannot pass a contact load to a joint limit or another internal row: a body resting on a link held at a limit sinks about ``(1 + M / m) * g * dt**2 / baumgarte`` into it at any iteration count, and a heavy enough body falls through. Use the default ``"interleaved"`` or ``"phased"`` schedule when contacts load internal rows.
