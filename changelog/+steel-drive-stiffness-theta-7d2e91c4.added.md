Add `SolverSteel(drive_stiffness_theta=...)`, the weight of the end-of-step position in the spring force of `drive_mode="augmented"` joint drives. The default `1.0` keeps backward Euler; `0.0` integrates the drive stiffness symplectically, as MuJoCo integrates position actuators, so a mechanism that flexes on impact and springs back keeps the energy its drives store instead of losing a share of it every step.
