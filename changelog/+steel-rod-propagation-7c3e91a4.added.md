Rod articulations now run under the propagation contact response of `SolverSteel`: each rod joint's material block enters the articulated-body factorization, pure rod trees take their unconstrained velocity from it instead of the dense mass matrix, loop closures (including rings closed by a rod joint) are solved as rows of the propagation sweep, and on CUDA rod trees walk dedicated factor, response and propagation kernels. The cable examples default to the propagation response with smaller substep and iteration budgets (`--rod-substeps` and `--rod-iterations` override them); the Franka pick-and-place and cross-slide table examples keep the immediate response. With `solve_mode="colored"`, worlds holding a single rod articulation solve the immediate response with the per-world fused sweep, so `coupled_contact_passes` applies in both solve modes. On CUDA, the serial propagation sweep solves each contact's normal row and sliding pair as one update with the world's twists in shared memory, when the world's bodies fit. Chain-shaped rod trees propagate and respond through warp scans over the chain, and with no more worlds than SMs the colored sweep spreads each world's colors over a wide block.
