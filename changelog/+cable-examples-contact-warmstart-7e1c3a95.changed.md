The cable examples warm-start their contacts under native Steel (`--solver steel`): `SolverSteel` matches each contact to its predecessor itself (by shape pair and body-frame anchors), so a held grasp or a cable resting on a surface keeps its load instead of rebuilding it every substep, and the collision pipelines need no contact matching. The examples size their contacts and Steel rows per world from each task's measured peak. The Franka cable pick-and-place now stops its fingers at the cable surface with no slip at its default budget, and the serial cable bundle keeps its cables in contact.
