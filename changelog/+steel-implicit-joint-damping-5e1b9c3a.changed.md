Integrate passive `Model.joint_damping` implicitly in `SolverSteel` by folding `dt * damping` into the mass-matrix diagonal, as for drive damping, in both drive modes. This removes the explicit damping step-size limit `dt * damping / m_eff < 2`, which chains of light links exceeded at modest damping; explicit springs and velocity-product forces still limit `dt`. Damped trajectories within the old limit change by the difference between explicit and implicit Euler damping, which is first order in `dt`. No migration is required.
