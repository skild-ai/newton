Integrate passive `Model.joint_damping` implicitly in `SolverSteel` by folding `dt * damping` into the mass-matrix diagonal, as for drive damping, in both drive modes. Damping beyond the explicit bound `dt * damping / m_eff < 2`, for example on chains of light links, no longer diverges; damped trajectories within that bound change by the difference between explicit and implicit Euler damping, which is first order in `dt`. No migration is required.
