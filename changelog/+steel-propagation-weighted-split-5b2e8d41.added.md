Add `propagation_weighted_split` to `SolverSteel`: with the propagation response, each articulation joint's mobility is shared among the row-bearing bodies below it in proportion to the normal impulse their rows carry at the start of the step, instead of evenly, and the shares still sum to one. A cable lying on a table carries rows on every link, so an even share left a grasped link about a tenth of its mobility and a pinch converged about 16 times slower than the exact response. With contact warm starting the loaded links take most of it, and the Franka cable grasp holds at about a quarter of the sweeps. Only rod articulations weigh their links, and a row between two links of one articulation takes the smaller of their shares. Without warm-started impulses every share stays even. It is on by default when the model has rod material.
