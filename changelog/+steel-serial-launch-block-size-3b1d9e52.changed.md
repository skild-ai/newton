Speed up ``SolverSteel`` with many worlds by sizing the blocks of its one-thread-per-articulation kernels (forward kinematics, inverse dynamics, drives, the loop Cholesky and triangular solves, row builders) from the thread and SM counts (8 to 64 threads per block) instead of launching 256 threads per block, so the articulations occupy many more SMs; results are bitwise unchanged. The ``serial_kernel_block_dim`` kernel override now defaults to ``"auto"`` and also accepts 8 and 16. No migration is required.
