Speed up ``SolverSteel`` with many worlds by sizing the blocks of its one-thread-per-articulation kernels (forward kinematics, inverse dynamics, drives, the loop Cholesky and triangular solves, row builders) to spread the articulations over every SM instead of launching 256 threads per block; results are bitwise unchanged. The ``serial_kernel_block_dim`` kernel override now defaults to ``"auto"`` and also accepts 8 and 16. No migration is required.
