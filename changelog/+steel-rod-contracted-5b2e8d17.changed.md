Rod chains under `SolverSteel`'s propagation response iterate only over their contact-active links: within a solve, a chain with few active links updates their twists from the impulses summed so far through the response maps between them, and the full propagation of the summed impulses runs once after the iterations. On CUDA, rod chains also take their forward kinematics and inverse-dynamics wrenches from per-joint kernels and short serial passes in shared memory instead of the serial joint walk, the rod factorization keeps only its recursion (child-independent terms before it, propagation records after it, five trees per warp for uniform groups at scale), the chain passes read one compact record and stage it in shared memory, and the propagation coloring colors with one warp over staged units and sorts in shared memory. Loop closures of the propagation response no longer count as internal rows. The cable examples enable `reuse_kinematics`.
